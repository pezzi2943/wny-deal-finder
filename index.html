How the scores work

Deal (0–100): rent-to-price, cash flow per door, cash-on-cash and cap rate, assuming 20% down at the current 30-yr rate plus an investor premium, estimated taxes, insurance, and 8% vacancy, 8% repairs, 5% capex, 8% management. Location (0–100): NY DCJS index-crime rate for the property's actual municipality, NYSED graduation and Regents-with-Advanced rates for its school district, and local median list $/sqft, ranked across all listings. Condition: the condition the numbers require (100 − Deal) vs an observed estimate from listing text, age and price/sqft. Style scores blend the three with different weights.

Disclaimer: All figures are automated estimates for informational purposes only and are not financial, legal or investment advice or an appraisal. Rents are third-party estimates; taxes are estimated from published full-value rates, not actual bills; condition is inferred from listing text, not an inspection. Listing data may be outdated or inaccurate. Verify everything independently before making any decision.
